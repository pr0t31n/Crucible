                Include Out-of-Scope — Separated Results
                
        
                This mode will test derived targets that share a parent domain or IP address with your explicitly defined in-scope targets. Results will be kept separate from in-scope findings.
                
        
                  
          
                • OOS findings will be tagged and visually distinguished in the Findings tab
                  • 
          
                • The report will include separate scope sections for OOS findings
                  • 
          
                • Derived targets that do not share a parent domain or IP with in-scope targets will not be tested
                  • 
        
                
      
                
      
                
        
                Include Out-of-Scope as In-Scope — Combined Results
                
        
                This mode will test derived targets and merge all findings with in-scope results. There will be no distinction between in-scope and OOS findings.
                
        
                  
          
                • All findings are treated identically regardless of whether the target was explicitly in-scope
                  • 
          
                • The report will include OOS findings in the same sections as in-scope findings
                  • 
          
                • Derived targets that do not share a parent domain or IP with in-scope targets will not be tested
                  • 
        
                
      
                
      
                
        Warning: Testing out-of-scope targets may violate your rules of engagement. Ensure you have explicit authorization to test derived targets before enabling this option.
